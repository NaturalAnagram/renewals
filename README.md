# Renewals

A simple tracker for the things in your life that expire — insurance, driver's licenses, passports,
vehicle tabs, leases — showing when each one expires and how to renew it.

**Use it:** https://naturalanagram.github.io/renewals/

## What it does
- **See what's due at a glance.** Every item gets a status from its expiry date: 🔴 Expired,
  🟠 Renew now (inside its reminder window), 🟡 Coming up, or 🟢 OK. Summary tiles at the top count
  each status, and tapping one filters the list.
- **Keep the renewal details in one place.** Each item can hold its provider, a renewal link (a
  one-tap **Renew ↗** button), a phone number (tap to call), and notes such as what to bring.
- **Mark renewed.** Moves the expiry date forward by the item's renewal cycle (6 months, 1 year,
  8 years and so on), so a renewed item goes back to OK.
- **Quick-start templates** for common items like auto insurance, a passport or vehicle tabs fill in
  the category, cycle and reminder for you.
- **Search and filter** by name or category; the list is sorted by whatever expires soonest.
- **Push reminders** on your phone or computer at 9 AM when an item enters its "remind me" window,
  then 7 days, 1 day and on the day it expires.
- **Sync across devices** by signing in with an emailed link. Signed out, everything stays in that
  browser only.
- **Backups and calendar export.** Export or import a backup file, or add every expiry date to your
  calendar (.ics).
- **Works on your phone.** Install it to your home screen like an app; it follows your light or
  dark theme.
